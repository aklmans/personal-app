# Let readers share a quote or highlight from an article

  ## What & Why
  The current share feature shares the whole article URL. Many readers want to share a specific passage that caught their attention. Highlight-to-share is a natural extension that drives more social sharing.

  ## Done looks like
  - Long-pressing text in the article WebView surfaces a "Share quote" option
  - Tapping it pre-fills the share sheet with the selected text and the article URL
  - Works on iOS and Android
